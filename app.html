Backup Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up what a backup plan stores and reads from the numbers you type. It has not seen your backup software, your data or your disks. It does not know about compression or deduplication, whether every incremental is the same size, whether your backups are checked, or whether a restore will work, and it gives no verdict that a plan protects your data. Every number is your own entry.

The space truth

A full copy every few days, incrementals on the other days, kept for a number of days.

The restore truth

The worst case restore reads the last full copy plus every incremental after it.

The reach truth

How far before a problem your restore points still go.

Backup Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
